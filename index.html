01 / INTRODUCTION

See more. Compute less.
Act in the real world.

Multi-view images help a robot understand its surroundings. Deploying that understanding on resource-constrained hardware calls for a more compact policy.

RTPolicy combines a frozen DINOv3-CNN visual backbone, alternating attention across camera views, and a spatial adapter. A flow-matching action head turns the resulting visual representation and robot state into continuous action sequences.

At 640 × 480 (W × H) resolution, three camera views produce 900 visual patch tokens. The adapter reduces them to 12 conditioning tokens, making the visual-to-action interface compact enough for edge deployment.

01

Understand across views

A shared pretrained backbone and alternating attention bring complementary camera observations together.

02

Keep the representation compact

Four visual tokens per camera preserve a spatial interface for the action head, instead of passing every image patch.

03

Take the policy to the edge

Four-step flow matching and INT16 / INT8 deployment are evaluated on S100 and S600 edge platforms.
